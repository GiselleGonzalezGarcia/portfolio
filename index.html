01 / In progress

The Cuba-Ireland Immigration Database (CIID)

The Cuba-Ireland Immigration Database was created to provide a scholarly resource that guarantees centralized access to information about the Irish diaspora to Cuba and promotes knowledge of their presence to a wider audience. This database centres individual migrants, recovering their names and stories from historical records while reconstructing their kinship and social connections in Cuba.

↗

02 / In progress

Redes Isleñas

Redes Isleñas is an open, collaborative public-history project on Canarian emigration to the Americas from the fifteenth to the twentieth century. It centres on a compilation of biographical, demographic and genealogical data on the migrants, in order to understand their family networks and other patterns that shaped their lives on both sides of the Atlantic Ocean.

03 / In progress

Knights Project

This project studies the networks of Irish knights in the Spanish religious military orders of Alcántara, Calatrava, Carlos III, Montesa, and Santiago from the seventeenth to the twentieth century.

01 / Collaborator

Cuba-Ireland Digital Archive (CIDA)

The Cuba-Ireland Digital Archive is a collection of eighteenth- and nineteenth-century sources from the Cuban National Archives in Havana. Using Irish surnames as key search terms, the sources are curated as a collection concerned with Irish migration and settlement in Cuba during the colonial period. Project led by Dr. Margaret Brehony.
